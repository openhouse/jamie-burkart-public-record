## Why it matters

The NYC Artist Coalition published a 31-second Story joining its call to Albany
and the New York City Council with an edited audio excerpt from Jamie's May 19,
2026 remarks at Wonderville. The excerpt connects the history and future of
cultural spaces to collective agency: people who make neighborhood life can
organize for the conditions that let those places endure.

The record offers a compact public example of Jamie's practice across cultural
organizing, tenant protection, neighborhood continuity, and the social value
of places where people can gather.

## Evidence boundary

The public Story establishes that the coalition account published the media
and that the audio includes words attributed to Jamie. It does not establish
that Jamie solely authored, edited, or published the Story; that tagged
accounts and sponsor acknowledgements establish endorsement; that every named
person supports every proposal; or that legislation passed or changed because
of this publication.

The diarized transcript was checked across local speech recognition, animated
Story captions, Jamie's supplied cut, and the source-event transcript. That
review restored the interleaved Joe Ahearn, Stephanie Gross, and room turns and
repaired small wording differences in Jamie's turns. Final human
listening/approval remains separate, so the checksum-bound canonical archive—not
this reference record—remains the source of truth for the media and transcript.
