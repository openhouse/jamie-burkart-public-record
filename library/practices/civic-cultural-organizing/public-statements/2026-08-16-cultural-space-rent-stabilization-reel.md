## Why it matters

The NYC Artist Coalition published a 31-second Reel that carries Jamie's May
19 Wonderville remarks into a durable public campaign object. His words join
the survival of small cultural spaces to collective agency and a direct call
for action at both the state and city levels. The full caption thanks Fair Rent
NYC's Assembly, Senate, and City Council sponsors, links the campaign solution,
and invites event organizers to meet in person.

The record shows a recurring part of Jamie's civic-cultural practice: connecting
the lived value of neighborhood gathering places to an actionable legislative
frame, while placing his own voice inside a larger coalition and movement.

The Reel carries the same decoded audio as the August 15 Story but remains a
distinct publication with its own URL, date, platform ID, visual treatment,
caption, and media-file checksum.

## Evidence boundary

The public source establishes coalition-account publication and the presence
of words attributed to Jamie in the edited audio. It does not establish that
Jamie solely authored, edited, or published the Reel. Its sponsor acknowledgements are coalition-account words, not newly recovered statements by each named official. Its invitation does not establish who later attended, spoke, consented, or endorsed legislation.
