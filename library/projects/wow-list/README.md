# WOW List as senior product-leadership evidence

## Conclusion

WOW List makes an unusually strong case for Jamie Burkart as a senior product
manager for resident-facing and public-interest technology. Jamie and Richard
Caceres co-built an operating community-calendar and discovery service rather
than a speculative concept. The public record connects product definition,
hands-on implementation, community discovery, adoption, communications,
instrumentation, and sustained stewardship.

For NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (OTI) Senior Product Manager Job ID
782366, this is a strong product-practice match. It does not guarantee an award
or establish that Jamie satisfies the posting's civil-service minimum
qualifications; those remain employer and human-review decisions.

## Demonstrated product practice

- **End-to-end delivery:** WOW List moved from calendars made through Sunday
  Dinner into a working multi-user service with profiles, editable events,
  followable interests and people, calendar integration, and personalized email.
- **Discovery and adoption:** Public support records describe joining, adding
  events, profiles, and followed calendars. The product was accompanied by
  facilitator, onboarding, editorial, and organizer-facing practices.
- **Product and technical judgment:** The service joined a social model of local
  relevance to an implemented application and production data system.
- **Measurement with restraint:** A public-safe July 2017 production snapshot
  documents historical product activity. Those counts are not current active
  users, retention, outcomes, or proof of causal impact.
- **Communication and institutional knowledge:** Public product support, weekly
  editorial communication, organizer guidance, and the present governed record
  show sustained attention to how people understand and continue a system.

## Why the match is unusually strong

The differentiator is the combination. Many records show either a technical
build or community work; WOW List shows a product whose usefulness depended on
both. Jamie worked near the code, the data model, the communication layer, and
the social practices through which people adopted and maintained the service.
That gives him direct experience with a central difficulty of public products:
making a system not only available, but intelligible, trustworthy, and usable
in people's existing lives.

The surviving record also permits mature product judgment after the fact. Jamie
can distinguish stored activity from outcomes, identify incomplete source
coverage, and preserve evidence without treating access as consent. That is a
meaningful qualification for work with imperfect data and public obligations.

## Explicit gaps and human gates

- The civil-service minimum qualifications require a complete education and
  employment review. WOW List cannot settle that eligibility question by
  itself.
- Historical WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 conformance have not been verified.
- WOW List alone does not establish formal government product ownership,
  procurement, agency legal or policy review, or executive-briefing authority.
- The record supports onboarding and distributed facilitation, not a claim of
  formal product hiring, personnel management, or PM mentorship.
- Hiring, reference checks, portfolio selection, publication, and final wording
  remain human decisions.

## Collective credit

Jamie and Richard Caceres are publicly credited as co-builders. Guest writers,
editors, list maintainers, facilitators, organizers, venues, and participants
also made the product socially operative. Leadership should be described as
building and stewarding conditions for distributed participation, never as
sole authorship of the platform or its community outcomes.
