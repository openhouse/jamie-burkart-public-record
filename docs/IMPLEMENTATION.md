- twelve canonical records across public testimony, a public archive project,
  and three encounter recovery leads;

- relative-link browse indexes;
- a content-addressed public knowledge manifest;
- deterministic validation and twelve adversarial mutations;

Public release, encounter narration, photo selection, and portfolio composition
remain separate human decisions.
