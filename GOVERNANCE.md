# Governance

## Authority layers

1. Private archival research retains protected correspondence, transcripts,
   exact artifacts, and evidence gaps.
2. This repository holds only public-safe source records and links to public
   canonical editions.
3. The `jamieburk.art` Knowledge Wiki may import a pinned manifest and derive
   bounded public-safe meaning.
4. Portfolio surfaces may selectively project only reviewed, purpose-fit
   claims.

## Human gates

- Jamie controls publication and portfolio selection.
- Speakers and writers control permission for non-public or newly quoted words.
- Rights holders control reproduction.
- Collaborators and institutions retain credit and official-capacity authority.
- Represented people require dignity, consent, caption, and destination review.

The initial implementation does not publish recommendation letters, private
course evaluations, private messages, or photographs. Every selected image
would remain `publication-review-required` even after technical checks.

## Corrections and withdrawals

Submit a correction through [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md). A correction may
change a local record or remove it from the public manifest without altering
the separately governed canonical source. Withdrawn material remains absent
from generated indexes after the next build.
