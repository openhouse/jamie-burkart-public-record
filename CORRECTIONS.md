# Corrections

Please open an issue or pull request identifying:

- the record ID and path;
- the exact statement needing correction;
- a public source supporting the change;
- whether the problem concerns transcription, attribution, context, credit,
  rights, date, relationship semantics, or withdrawal.

Urgent privacy or safety concerns should be reported without reproducing the
sensitive material in a public issue. Repository maintainers may remove a
record from generated indexes while the underlying question is reviewed.
